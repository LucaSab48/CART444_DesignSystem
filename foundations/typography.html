Foundation 06

Typography

Typography creates hierarchy through size, weight, spacing, and rhythm. This system uses a clear set of role-based text styles: display, headline, title, body, label, and legal.

Type roles

Display

Design Systems Shape Experience

Headline

Foundation pages define the visual language

Title

Typography organizes content into readable layers.

Body

Body text is used for longer explanations, contextual descriptions, and content throughout the design system documentation.

Label

Used for small interface labels and metadata.

Legal

Used for notes, captions, and small system text.

  --text-headline: clamp(2rem, 3vw, 3rem);

  --text-body: 1rem;
  --text-label: 0.875rem;
  --text-legal: 0.75rem;
