Foundation 08

Icons

Icons use a simple stroke-based style. They sit on a 24px grid, use a 2px stroke, and remain visually lightweight to support content rather than dominate it.

Icon style

Guidelines

Property Rule
Size 24px default
Stroke 2px
Style Monoline, minimal, geometric
Use Navigation, metadata, small interface cues
