Design System

This design system is the scaffold for my portfolio website. It adopts structural principles from modern design systems and adapts them to a visual language centered on clarity, atmosphere, and strong hierarchy.

Approach

Adopt, then adapt visually

What I adopted

A 12-column grid, an 8-point spacing system, responsive breakpoints, layered hierarchy, and standardized typography roles.

What I adapted

Typography choices, color palette, image treatment, icon tone, and the overall mood of the interface to fit my creative practice.
